My Lecture

I had the opportunity to give a lecture on the topic: NON-TECHNICAL SKILLS IN TECH, and I focused on the sub-topic; PROBLEM SOLVING. I had a lot of positive feedback and after my lecture we were able to solve a live problem we were encountering at the moment in the company which was How to power and wire a total of about 12 CCTV cameras to be installed 2-5 km away from each other. The solution was to use solar panels to power the camers and use an optic fiber wire to run to the control station for video feed and transmit the others to the control center.
